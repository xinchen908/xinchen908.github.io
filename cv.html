Academic background

Prior academic appointments include the James C. Edenfield Chair and Professor at Georgia Tech and faculty positions at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

The archived CV records a postdoctoral appointment at MIT (2003–2004) and appointment to the Illinois faculty in 2004, with promotion to Associate Professor in 2011 and Professor in 2015.
