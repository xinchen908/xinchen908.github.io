Questions, structure, decisions

Research

Optimization and learning for operations: understanding the structure of decision problems and developing policies for uncertain environments.

01 /

Optimization & mathematical structure

Convexity and discrete convex analysis reveal structure in operations models. Research topics include M-natural-convexity, S-convexity, supermodularity and random quadratic optimization.

Related work
Exactness of the DNN Relaxation for Random Standard Quadratic Programs →S-Convexity and Gross Substitutability →M-Natural-Convexity and Its Applications in Operations →Discrete Convex Analysis and Its Applications in Operations: A Survey →
02 /

Inventory & supply chain management

Inventory policies, lead-time uncertainty, dual sourcing and joint inventory-pricing decisions connect stochastic models with supply chain operations.

Related work
Optimality Gap of Tailored Base-Surge Policies Decays Exponentially in Regular-Source Lead Times for Dual-Sourcing Models →Learning in Lost-Sales Inventory Systems with Stochastic Lead Times and Random Supplies →Asymptotic Optimality of Semi-Open-Loop Policies in Inventory Models with Stochastic Lead Times →Asymptotic optimality of constant-order policies in joint pricing and inventory control models →
03 /

Revenue management & dynamic pricing

Revenue management, assortment optimization and dynamic pricing address how firms coordinate product choices, capacity, prices and inventory.

Related work
Joint Assortment Optimization and Discrete Marketing Mix Allocation →Assortment Competition in a Two-Tier Supply Chain Under Cardinality Constraints →Efficient Algorithms for a Class of Stochastic Hidden Convex Optimization and Its Applications in Network Revenue Management →Efficient Algorithms for Dynamic Pricing Problem with Reference Price Effect →
04 /

Learning & stochastic control

Policy optimization and learning methods support sequential decision-making in Markov decision processes and nonstationary inventory systems.

Related work
Learning When to Restart: Nonstationary Newsvendor from Uncensored to Censored Demand →A Minimax-MDP Framework with Future-imposed Conditions for Learning-augmented Problems →Landscape of Policy Optimization for Finite Horizon MDPs with General State and Action →Multi-level Monte-Carlo Gradient Methods for Stochastic Optimization with Biased Oracles →
05 /

Data analytics & stochastic optimization

Stochastic optimization and data-driven models provide methods for learning from uncertain observations and making decisions with imperfect information.

Related work
On the Bias-Variance-Cost Tradeoff of Stochastic Optimization →Biased Stochastic Gradient Descent for Conditional Stochastic Optimization →Sample Complexity of Sample Average Approximation for Conditional Stochastic Optimization →
